1. **`MATH 03 & 04`**: Grouped into a single document file (`Math 03-01 to Math 04-05`).

## 2. ELECTRONICS ENGINEERING (ELECS)

*(Continuous sequence `ELEC 01` through `ELEC 15` — **Zero Gaps**)*

- **`ELEC 01 - Electricity and Magnetism`** (`01-01` to `01-06`): Coulomb's Law, Electric Field, Gauss's Law, Magnetic Flux, Ampere's Law, Electromagnetic Induction.
- **`ELEC 02 - Electrical Elements`** (`02-01` to `02-03`): Resistors (Color Codes, Temperature Coefficient), Capacitors (Dielectrics, Parasitics), Inductors (Self/Mutual).
- **`ELEC 03 - DC Circuits`** (`03-01` to `03-06`): Ohm's Law, Kirchhoff's Laws, Mesh/Nodal Analysis, Thevenin, Norton, Superposition, Maximum Power Transfer.
- **`ELEC 04 - AC Circuits`** (`04-01` to `04-04`): Sinusoids, Phasors, Complex Impedance, Series/Parallel RLC, Power Triangle (Real, Reactive, Apparent Power, Power Factor Correction).
- **`ELEC 05 - Transients and Resonant Circuits`** (`05-01` to `05-05`): RC/RL First-Order Time Constants, RLC Second-Order Damping, Series/Parallel Resonance, Q-Factor, Bandwidth.
- **`ELEC 06 - Semiconductor Physics and Diodes`** (`06-01` to `06-04`): PN Junctions, Energy Bands, Diode Models, Zener Diodes, Rectifiers, Voltage Multipliers, Clippers & Clampers.
- **`ELEC 07 - Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJT)`** (`07-01` to `07-03`): CE, CB, CC Configurations, DC Biasing (Fixed, Self, Voltage Divider), AC Small-Signal Models, h-Parameters.
- **`ELEC 08 - Field Effect Transistors (FET and MOSFET)`** (`08-01` to `08-04`): JFET Characteristics, Shockley's Equation, D-MOSFET and E-MOSFET Biasing, Transconductance, CMOS Inverters.
- **`ELEC 09 - Operational Amplifiers (Op-Amps)`** (`09-01` to `09-03`): Ideal Op-Amp Characteristics, Inverting/Non-Inverting Amplifiers, Summing, Integrator, Differentiator, Active Filters.
- **`ELEC 10 - Industrial Electronics`** (`10-01` to `10-03`): Thyristors (SCR, TRIAC, DIAC), Phase Control, Optocouplers, Relays, Transducers, Industrial Motor Drives.
- **`ELEC 11 - Power Supplies`** (`11-01` to `11-04`): Linear Voltage Regulators (78xx/79xx, LM317), SMPS (Buck, Boost, Buck-Boost), Ripple Factor, Heat Sink Design.
- **`ELEC 12 - Microelectronics`** (`12-01` to `12-03`): Semiconductor Fabrication, Photolithography, Diffusion, Ion Implantation, CMOS IC Layout & Packaging.
- **`ELEC 13 - Test and Measurement`** (`13-01` to `13-04`): Analog/Digital Multimeters, Oscilloscope Operation & Probing, AC/DC Bridge Circuits (Wheatstone, Maxwell, Wien), Spectrum Analyzers.
- **`ELEC 14 - Feedback and Oscillators`** (`14-01` to `14-03`): Negative Feedback Topologies, Barkhausen Criterion, LC/RC Oscillators (Hartley, Colpitts, Wien Bridge, Crystal).
- **`ELEC 15 - Digital Electronics`** (`15-01` to `15-06`): Number Systems (Binary, Hex, Octal), Logic Gates, Karnaugh Maps, Adders, Multiplexers, Flip-Flops, Counters & Shift Registers.

## 3. GENERAL ENGINEERING & APPLIED SCIENCES (GEAS)

- **`GEAS 01 - Chemistry for Engineers`** (`01-01` to `01-13`): Atomic Structure, Periodic Trends, Chemical Bonding, Stoichiometry, Gases, Thermochemistry, Electrochemistry, Nuclear Chemistry.
- **`GEAS 02 - Physics 1 (Mechanics, Waves, Sound)`** (`02-01` to `02-14`): Vectors, Kinematics, Newton's Laws, Work-Energy-Power, Impulse-Momentum, Collisions, Rotational Dynamics, Periodic Motion, Wave Motion, Sound, Optics (Mirrors & Lenses).
- **`GEAS 03 - Physics 2 (Electricity, Magnetism, Modern Physics)`** (`03-01` to `03-13`): Electric Charges, Electric Potential, Capacitance, Magnetism, Induction, Quantum Physics, Relativity, Thermodynamics (Zeroth, 1st & 2nd Laws, Heat Engines, Entropy).
- **`GEAS 04 - Engineering Mechanics & Strength of Materials`** (`04-01` to `04-17`): Concurrent Forces, Lami's Theorem, Trusses, Beams, Friction, Belt Friction, Centroids, Moments of Inertia, Kinematics, Projectile Motion, Circular Motion, Banking of Curves, Stress-Strain, Torsion, Beam Deflection.
- **`GEAS 05 - Thermodynamics`** (`05-01` to `05-07`): Pure Substances, Ideal Gases, First Law for Closed/Open Systems, Second Law, Carnot Cycle, Heat Transfer (Conduction, Convection, Radiation).
- **`GEAS 06 - Engineering Economics`** (`06-01` to `06-06`): Simple & Compound Interest, Annuities, Amortization, Depreciation (Straight-Line, Sinking Fund, DDB, SOYD), Capitalized Cost, Rate of Return, Benefit-Cost Ratio.
- **`GEAS 09 - Electromagnetics`**: Maxwell's Equations, Vector Calculus, Plane Wave Propagation, Poynting Vector, Skin Effect, Reflection & Refraction.
- **`GEAS 10 - ECE Laws and Ethics`**: Republic Act 9292 (Electronics Engineering Law of 2004), Code of Ethics, Contracts & Obligations, Philippine Electronics Code (PEC).
- **`GEAS 11 - Material Science and Engineering`**: Crystal Structures, Miller Indices, Phase Diagrams, Iron-Carbon System, Mechanical Testing (Rockwell, Brinell, Charpy), Polymers, Ceramics.
- **`GEAS 12 - Computer Programming`**: Flowcharts, Algorithms, C/C++ Syntax, Control Structures, Functions, Arrays, Pointers, Basic Data Structures.
- **`GEAS 13 - Environmental Science and Engineering`**: Ecology, Air/Water Pollution Control, Environmental Impact Assessment, Solid Waste Management, Climate Change.
- **`GEAS 14 - Technopreneurship 101`**: Business Model Canvas, Intellectual Property (Patents, Trademarks, Copyrights), Venture Capital, Startup Operations.

#### ⚠️ Gaps in GEAS:
* **`GEAS 07 & 08`**: Missing from standalone questionnaire naming. In PRC syllabi, these traditionally correspond to *Fluid Mechanics* and *Engineering Management* (which were folded into GEAS 04/05 and GEAS 14).

## 4. ELECTRONICS SYSTEMS & TECHNOLOGIES (EST)

*(Continuous sequence `EST 01` through `EST 10` — **Zero Gaps**)*

- **`EST 01 - Fundamentals of Communications`** (`01-01` to `01-06`): Signal Spectra, Decibels ($dBm, dBu, dBW$), Thermal Noise, Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR), Noise Figure & Noise Temperature.
- **`EST 02 - Radiowave Propagation`** (`02-01` to `02-03`): Ground Wave, Sky Wave (Ionospheric Layers, MUF, LUF, Critical Frequency), Space Wave (Radio Horizon, Ducting), Fading.
- **`EST 03 - Analog Modulation`**: AM (DSB-FC, DSB-SC, SSB, VSB, Modulation Index, Power Calculations), FM & PM (Deviation, Bessel Functions, Carson's Bandwidth, Pre-emphasis/De-emphasis).
- **`EST 04 - Transmission Lines`**: Primary/Secondary Constants, Characteristic Impedance ($Z_0$), Voltage Reflection Coefficient ($\Gamma$), Standing Wave Ratio (SWR), Smith Chart Impedance Matching.
- **`EST 05 - Antennas`**: Isotropic Radiator, Dipoles, Radiation Resistance, Directivity & Gain ($dBi, dBd$), Effective Isotropic Radiated Power (EIRP), Antenna Arrays, Parabolic Dishes.
- **`EST 06 - Microwave Communications`**: Waveguides (Rectangular, Circular, Cutoff Frequencies), Radar (Radar Equation, Pulse Radar), Satellite Communications (Orbital Altitudes, Link Budgets).
- **`EST 07 - Optical Fiber Communications`**: Snell's Law, Total Internal Reflection, Numerical Aperture (NA), Single-Mode vs Multi-Mode Fibers, Attenuation (1310nm/1550nm Windows), Optical Sources (LED/Laser).
- **`EST 08 - Telephony`**: PSTN, Local Loop, 2-Wire to 4-Wire Hybrids, Signaling (DTMF, SS7), Traffic Engineering (Erlang B & Erlang C Formulas).
- **`EST 09 - Digital Communications`**: Sampling Theorem (Nyquist Rate), Quantization Noise, Pulse Code Modulation (PCM), Digital Modulation Schemes (ASK, FSK, BPSK, QPSK, 8-PSK, 16-QAM).
- **`EST 10 - Data Communications`**: OSI 7-Layer Model, TCP/IP Protocol Suite, Framing, Error Detection (Parity, Checksum, CRC-16/32), Ethernet Standards (IEEE 802.3), IP Addressing & Subnetting.
