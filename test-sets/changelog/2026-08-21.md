# Changelog: 2026-08-21

## Test Set Naming Convention & Standardization
- Published [naming_convention_specification.md](file:///c:/Users/reyna/OneDrive/Documents/marnie_quiz/test-sets/naming_convention_specification.md) defining a standardized, extensible 5-part naming grammar:
  ```
  [subject]_[topic-code]_[topic-name]_[test-type]_[subtopic-scope]_[set-number].csv
  ```
- **Pedagogical Tiers Defined**:
  - `diagnostic` (formerly *pretest*, 30 items): Pre-assessment & retrieval baseline.
  - `review` (formerly *test*, 25 items): 1:1 official review questionnaire transcription.
  - `drill` (formerly *shorttest*, 10 items): High-velocity concept and formula checks.
  - `simulation` (formerly *longtest*, 50 items): Full-spectrum comprehensive mock exam.
- **Subtopic Scoping & Multiplicity**:
  - Integrated `[subtopic-scope]` parameter (`all` vs. focused ranges like `01-03`, `04-06`, `08-14`).
  - Integrated `[set-number]` parameter (`set01`, `set02`, `set03`, etc.) to support multiple test sets of the same tier and scope.
