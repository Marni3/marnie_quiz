# Changelog: 2026-08-26

## Summary of Progress & Architectural Decisions

### 1. Expanded In-Line Concept Checks Capacity
- **Exhaustive Active-Recall Scaling:**
  - Upgraded concept check specifications in `SKILL.md`, `LEARNING_MODULE_AUTHORING_TAKEOVER_GUIDE.md`, and `MODULE_RESTRUCTURING_AND_PEDAGOGICAL_REFINEMENT_GUIDE.md` to support 5, 6, or up to 10+ multiple-choice questions per concept block/module.
  - Ensures deep retention coverage across all transcribed definitions, properties of numbers, and arithmetic rules.

### 2. Spaced Repetition System (SRS) Module Tracking Roadmap
- **Module Review State Tracking (`last_reviewed_at`):**
  - Planned database integration for tracking when a student last studied/reviewed each module.
  - The SRS review queue will expand beyond custom question sets to also recommend specific due modules and paired quiz sets for active recall based on memory decay curves (even for topics with high prior confidence).

### 3. V1 Reviewer Notes Grounding & Multimodal Image-Only Protocol
- Primary learning modules will directly transcribe review center lecture notes (e.g., Excel Review Center) in `Reference Documents/` 1-to-1 without artificial length caps.
- Strict requirement to convert PDF pages to PNG images in `scratch/` and read them exclusively with multimodal visual tools to preserve column layouts, diagrams, tables, and exact notation.
