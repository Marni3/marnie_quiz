  Introduction 
Integral Calculus is the branch of calculus 
which deals with functions to be 
integrated. Integration is the reverse 
process of differentiation. The function to 
be integrated is referred to as the 
integrand while the result of an 
integration is called integral. 
 
The integral symbol or sign ∫ , is an 
elongated S denoting sum (Latin: 
summa), was introduced by Leibniz, who 
named integral calculus as calculus 
summatorius.
