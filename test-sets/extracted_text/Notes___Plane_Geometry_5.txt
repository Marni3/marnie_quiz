Theorem involving a circle 
1. If a central angle and a peripheral 
angle are subtended by the same arc, 
then the central angle is twice as 
large as the peripheral angle. 
 
2. Inscribed angles subtend the same 
arc are equal. 
 
3. Inscribed angles subtended by the 
diameter of a circle are right angles.
