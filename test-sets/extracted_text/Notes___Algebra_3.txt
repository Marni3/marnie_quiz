The coefficients of a binomial expansion

arranging them in a triangular array or 
pattern. This is known as Pascal’s Triangle 
named after the famous French 
mathematician Blaise Pascal (1623 – 
1662). 
 
Each number in the triangle is equal to the 
sum of the two numbers immediately 
above it. 
 
In Italy, this triangular pattern is known as 
Tartaglia’s triangle while in many parts of 
Asia, it is referred to as Yang Hui’s 
triangle.
