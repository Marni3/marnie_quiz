Other Properties of 
Complex Numbers 
 
1. Equality of Complex Numbers 
 
 Two complex numbers a + jb and c + 
jd are equal if and only if a = c and b = 
d. 
 
2.  Conjugate of Complex Numbers 
 
 The conjugate of the complex number 
a + jb is a – jb. 
 
3. Multiplicative Inverse (Reciprocal) 
of Complex Numbers 
 
 The reciprocal or the multiplicative 
inverse of the complex number a + jb 
is defined as 1/(a + jb).

Matrices 
Matrix is a rectangular array of real 
numbers arranged in m rows and n 
columns. The term “matrix” was 
introduced by the English mathematician 
James Joseph Sylvester (1814 – 1897) in 
1850. The size of a matrix is determined 
by the number of rows and columns. The 
expression “m x n” is the dimension or 
order of the matrix. The first non-zero 
entry in a row of a matrix is known as the 
leading entry or the leading element.
