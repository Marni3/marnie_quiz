 Difference  
The difference of two sets A and B (also 
known as the set-theoretic difference of 
A and B, or the relative complement of B 
in A) is the set of elements that are in A 
but not in B. This is written A - B, or 
sometimes A\ B.

Relation and Function 
A relation is any association or link between 
elements of one set, called the domain or (less 
formally) the set of inputs, and another set, called 
the range or set of outputs.  
 
A function is a relation that has exactly one output 
for every possible input in the domain.
